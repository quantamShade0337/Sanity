The first public release of Sanity: a menu bar utility that shows how your Mac is doing at a glance, adds a Dynamic Island to the notch, and gathers everyday tools in one place.

## Install

1. Download **Sanity-1.0.0.dmg** below, open it and drag Sanity to Applications.
2. Open Sanity. macOS says it can't verify the app, because Sanity isn't notarized by Apple. Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Sanity.

You only do this once. Sanity needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. The Dynamic Island needs a Mac with a notch.

## What's in it

- **Dashboard:** one line on whether your Mac is fine, plus processor, memory, battery and storage, recent activity and what is using your Mac right now.
- **Dynamic Island:** volume, brightness, charging, AirPods connecting, Keep Awake, copies and saved captures appear in the notch, sized to fit what they show.
- **Tools:** clipboard history, screenshots and screen recording, window layout, an app switcher, a command bar, text snippets, cleanup and uninstalling, Homebrew, mouse and keyboard tweaks, and more. Each one is optional.
- **13 languages.**

## Good to know

- Sanity asks for each permission only when you turn on a feature that needs it.
- Fan Control isn't available in this release.
- Automatic updates are off. New versions are published here.
- Found a bug? [Open an issue](https://github.com/quantamShade0337/Sanity/issues/new).
